# Funding and support policy

## Current state

GetMyIP receives **no money from anyone**.

- No donation links
- No sponsors
- No affiliate or referral links
- No GitHub Sponsor button (this repository has no `FUNDING.yml`)

## Rules

- Money or sponsorship from companies is not accepted, in particular from VPN providers, IP data services and advertising companies.
- If this policy ever changes, the change is published here **before** any money is accepted, and every source and amount is listed publicly in this file.

## Projects we support

GetMyIP does not receive anything from these projects. We support them.

- **The Tor Project**: for its mission for a free and open internet. https://www.torproject.org
